statement

Megan Bates is a multi-disciplinary artist working in London and a member of the In-Grid artist collective.

Alongside her practice, she works as a software developer in climate-focussed energy systems modelling at transition zero.

She creates work using mediums such as programming, electronics, and 3D-modelling, alongside more traditional materials.

Her work primarily reflects on our relationship with technology, through trans*feminism, environmentalism, and scientific methodology.
